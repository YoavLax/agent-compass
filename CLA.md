## Why this exists

The project is licensed to the public under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE), and separately to
commercial users under negotiated terms agreed directly with the copyright
holders.

Offering a project under two sets of terms is only possible if one party
holds sufficient rights over all of it. Without this agreement, every
contribution would be licensed to the project under the public terms alone,
and the project could not include that contribution in a commercial license
— which in practice means it could not accept the contribution at all.

You keep the copyright in your work. You are granting a license, not
handing ownership over. You remain free to use, publish, sell, or relicense
your own contribution anywhere else, on any terms you like.

## How to agree

Add a `Signed-off-by` line to each commit:

```
git commit -s -m "your message"
```

which appends:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Signing off certifies that you have read this agreement and accept it for
that contribution. Use your real name and an email address attached to your
GitHub account.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, confirm first that they
permit it, or have them sign a corporate agreement. See §6.

## Note on legal review

This document is adapted from a standard template and has not been reviewed
by a lawyer on behalf of this project. It is used because a project offering
both public and commercial terms needs a rights grant on record from every
contributor, and having a reasonable agreement in place from the start is
better than discovering later that it is missing. Before relying on it in a
commercial transaction, have counsel review it.
